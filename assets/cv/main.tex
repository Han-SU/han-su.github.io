\begin{rSection}{Research Interests}
	My primary research focus is on the \textbf{formal design of Cyber-Physical Systems}, particularly when these systems interact with complex environments, such as those characterized by \textbf{delays} and \textbf{stochastic}, or when the control objectives are intricately defined, such as those specified using \textbf{Signal Temporal Logic}.
	I also have a keen interest in \textbf{lightweight formal methods (runtime monitoring/enforcement)} and \textbf{verification of probabilistic programs}.
	Currently, I am actively engaged in research dedicated to synthesizing enforcers for reactive systems against Signal Temporal Logic.
\end{rSection}
